# YouTube growth — agent index

Compact index of `DIRECTORY.md` (researched Nov 2026). [§N] = section N there, with sources. YouTube changes often: before acting on a threshold, date or %, open the section's source link.

## DECIDE (start here)
- New channel, no idea what to make → pick ONE audience, use the outlier method [§2], package first [§3], hook [§4], review weekly [§9]. Judge after ~50 videos / ~2 years (Paddy Galloway).
- Views low → which step failed? Few clicks vs your typical video = packaging [§3]; early drop-off = hook/pacing [§4]; no returning viewers = series, playlists, end screens [§8][§11].
- Shorts or long-form? Shorts = cheap discovery, low pay; long-form = income. Link each Short to a long video [§6].
- Faceless / AI channel → allowed if original; templated or reused = demonetised [§7].
- Money → YPP tiers, 2027 change, other streams [§10]. Strike or claim → [§12]. Automating with agents/APIs → [§13].
- Deep production craft (AI narration/TTS delivery and takes, Flow/Veo/Nano Banana quirks, documentary scripts, sound design and mixing) → sister skill `video-craft` (Video Craft Directory).

## §1 How recommendations work
- Ranked per viewer when they open the app, for long-term satisfaction; nothing is "pushed" to subscribers. ~90% of subscribers skip even your best videos (Todd Beaupré, Sep 2026).
- Signals: watch/search history, subs, likes, dislikes, "Not interested", satisfaction surveys. No single metric; weights vary by device (time on TV, likes on phones). TV favours long videos and series.
- New videos reach non-subscribers in the first hour; some videos find their audience a month+ later.
- CTR is relative: it falls as reach widens; compare with your own similar videos.
- Since Aug 24, 2026 public views count from the first frame; "engaged views" still drive earnings, CTR, AVD, retention.
- One channel per audience. Breaks or one weak video don't punish a channel.

## §2 Ideas and research
- Outlier = video doing 5–100× its channel's usual views → the idea drove it.
- Outlier method: list 20–50 niche/adjacent channels → sort by views vs channel average → note idea, title and thumbnail pattern → remix with your angle → test the package before filming.
- Sources: comments, autocomplete, competitors' outliers, adjacent niches, Studio Research/Insights tabs (Inspiration tab phased out for Ask Studio).
- Tools: 1of10, vidIQ (~$19/mo), ViewStats (~$49.99), OutlierKit, TubeBuddy.
- Filter: one thumbnail + title <60 chars? outlier proof? better/different? fits audience? evergreen vs timely.

## §3 Packaging (titles and thumbnails)
- Title + thumbnail before production (MrBeast). Draft 10 titles, 3 thumbnails (Galloway).
- vidIQ 2026 breakouts: 69% human face, 56% high contrast, ~5 words of text, few extreme expressions.
- Thumbnail: 1280×720, under 2 MB, JPG/PNG/GIF; one subject, 2–3 elements, text adds to title; check at phone size.
- Title: one promise, front-loaded, ~60 chars. Over-promising → early exits.
- Test & Compare: up to 3 titles/thumbnails; winner by watch time share; desktop only; not for Shorts or made-for-kids.

## §4 Scripting, hooks, retention
- First 30 s confirm the promise. Retention "Intro" = % still watching at 30 s.
- Hook: open on payoff/stakes, no "welcome back"; script first lines word for word.
- Hook-deliver cycle through the body; structure: hook → setup → escalating segments → payoff → fast ending pointing to one next video.
- Benchmarks (vidIQ): ~30% average percentage viewed; ~60% at 30 s. Your own average is the baseline.

## §5 Production, narration, sound
- Audio > camera. Mic close, quiet soft room; peaks ~−6 dB; master to −14 LUFS, true peak ~−1 dB.
- Music: YouTube Audio Library is safe; "royalty-free" can still get Content ID claims; Creator Music (US); paid libraries (Epidemic Sound, Artlist).
- Auto dubbing: 27 languages, 8 with Expressive Speech; multi-language audio gave pilot creators 25%+ watch time from other languages (Jamie Oliver views tripled).
- Edit: cut pauses, visual change every few seconds; DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Descript. Light at 45°, camera at eye level.

## §6 Shorts
- Up to 3 minutes (since Oct 15, 2024). Seed audience; Studio shows "Shown in feed" and viewed vs swiped away. Best performers ~70–90% viewed (Galloway, 3.3B views).
- First frame = hook; one complete idea; captions in the safe area; loopable end. No ideal length; deleting and reposting can be treated as spam.
- Since Oct 1, 2026 the feed reduces reach for re-uploads of others' content.
- No end screens or description links → use the Related video link to a long-form video.
- Pay: monthly per-country pool; music share comes out first; split by engaged views; creator keeps 45%. Shorts >1 min with a Content ID claim are blocked.
- From Feb 1, 2027 Shorts revenue sharing needs 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days.

## §7 Faceless and AI-assisted
- Allowed: AI as a creative tool, your own story and commentary. Not monetisable: generic/repetitive templates, slideshows, scrolling text, reused clips without added value (judged channel-wide, even with permission), AI personas posing as health/legal/financial/political experts.
- "Inauthentic content" policy: renamed from "repetitious" July 15, 2025; clarified July 2026.
- Disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content at upload (AI-use setting); labels since May 2026 also use YouTube's own detection.
- Jan 2026 crackdown: 16 channels, 35M subscribers removed (reported).
- Self-test: watch 3 videos with sound off — if indistinguishable, it's templated.

## §8 Upload and discovery settings
- Chapters: first timestamp 00:00, at least 3, each ≥10 s.
- End screens: last 5–20 s, video ≥25 s, up to 4 elements; not on made-for-kids. Cards mid-video.
- Tags matter little (misspellings). Captions, translated titles, playlists/series.
- Hype: viewers hype up to 3 long-form videos/week, video <7 days old, YPP channels with 500–500,000 subscribers; smaller channels get bonus points.
- Checklist: title/thumbnail + Test & Compare, description, chapters, audience setting, AI and paid-promotion disclosures, end screen, playlist, Related video link, schedule.

## §9 Analytics
- Chose it? impressions CTR, traffic sources → packaging. Stayed? AVD, APV, retention key moments (intro, spikes, dips) → hook/pacing. Came back? returning viewers, subs per video, end screen click rate.
- Compare against your own typical range at 24 h and 7 days; don't delete flops, re-package them.
- Weekly 30-min routine: list uploads → best/worst cause → study best video's spikes → one change next week.

## §10 Monetisation
- Fan-funding tier: 500 subs, 3 uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch hours/12 mo or 3M Shorts views/90 days → memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, Shopping.
- Ad revenue tier: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/12 mo or 10M Shorts views/90 days.
- From Feb 1, 2027 new creators: 8,000 watch hours/365 days or 20M Shorts views/90 days; existing partners stay in (announced Aug 10, 2026).
- Splits: long-form ads 55%, Shorts pool 45%, Premium share, fan funding 70%.
- RPM (what you earn per 1,000 views) ≠ CPM (advertiser price). Varies by niche, country, length (8+ min mid-rolls), season (Q4 high).
- Other streams: Shopping affiliate (500+ subs since Mar 2026, supported countries), own products, sponsors/BrandConnect (tick paid promotion), affiliate links, services.

## §11 Growth and community
- One audience per channel. Stages: 0–1k find what works; 1k–10k repeat it as series; 10k–100k team and systems; 100k+ expand.
- Reply in the first hour, pin a comment, ideas from comments; Posts and polls to test ideas; collaborations with overlapping audiences; an email list you own; avoid burnout.

## §12 Policies and risks
- Community Guidelines: first = warning (expires 90 days after optional training); strikes = 1-week then 2-week upload freeze; 3 strikes in 90 days = termination.
- Copyright strike = valid takedown; expires in 90 days; first needs Copyright School; 3 = termination. Deleting the video doesn't remove a strike.
- Content ID claim ≠ strike: block, track or take revenue; dispute or remove the part.
- Fair use is case by case (not legal advice). Made for kids (COPPA): no personalised ads, comments, end screens, notifications; set honestly.
- Also: misleading thumbnails/titles, fake engagement (bought views, sub4sub), paid promotion disclosure, advertiser-friendly limits (yellow icon).

## §13 Agents and Claude for YouTube
- Data API v3: 10,000 units/day default, plus separate buckets of 100 search.list and 100 videos.insert calls/day (updated Nov 12, 2026); more quota needs an audit. Uploads from unverified API projects stay private.
- Analytics API (owner OAuth, read-only) and Reporting API (bulk CSVs).
- MCP servers: community wrappers (e.g. ZubeidHendricks/youtube-mcp-server) and hosted Zernio; read the code, least-privilege OAuth scopes. Transcript scrapers may break or conflict with YouTube's terms.
- Pipelines: outlier research, title/thumbnail ideas, script research + fact-check, chapters/description, weekly analytics report, comment triage, Shorts repurposing. Human keeps: idea choice, final package, voice, publishing.
- Agent rules: never publish, delete or change monetisation without approval; no fake engagement or mass near-identical videos; disclose AI and sponsors; cite facts; track quota.
- Scheduling/hooks/subagents: see the Claude automation directory (skill `claude-automation-patterns`).
